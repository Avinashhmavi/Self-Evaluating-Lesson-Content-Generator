# Source 1: Lewis et al. 2020, "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" (arXiv 2005.11401, NeurIPS 2020)
- Large pretrained language models store facts in their parameters, but have limited ability to access and precisely change that knowledge.
- Open problems named: showing the source of a model's answers, and updating its world knowledge.
- RAG combines two kinds of memory: parametric memory (a pretrained sequence-to-sequence model) and non-parametric memory (a dense vector index of Wikipedia, searched by a pretrained neural retriever).
- Two formulations: one uses the same retrieved passages for the whole answer, the other can use different passages for each token.
- Reported results: state of the art on three open-domain question answering tasks; more specific, diverse and factual text than a parametric-only baseline.
